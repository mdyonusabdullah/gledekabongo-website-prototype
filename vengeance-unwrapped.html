The Story

You destroyed my family. Now I’ll destroy yours.

Dr. Abbie Rambally thought her nightmare ended when her kidnapped son was rescued seven years ago. But now anonymous presents prove someone hasn’t forgotten—and hasn’t forgiven.

Each beautifully wrapped gift contains a piece of her past: memories she’s tried to forget, secrets she’s buried deep. They know what happened at Yale. They hold her responsible for destroying an entire family. And they want her to pay the ultimate price.

As a psychologist, Abbie knows how obsession works. As a mother, she knows what fear feels like, and with each passing day, the gifts grow more terrifying.
