Fatal Match

She built a matchmaking empire on her Harvard credentials and a near perfect algorithm. Then she matched a monster.

Dr. Sabrina Ambrose built a matchmaking empire helping the rich and powerful find love. But when she discovers that one of her most sought-after bachelors has been secretly destroying the successful women she matches him with, she vows to expose him—until his murder turns her into the prime suspect and forces her to uncover which of his many victims struck first.

02 Award-Winning Thriller Recognized for Excellence · 3X Award Finalist

The Forbidden Heiress

There are two ways to bury secrets. Six feet under, or beneath billions of dollars.

Brilliant cancer researcher Sabree Warner is thrilled when she's recruited by the powerful CEO of Montague pharma. But when she uncovers a chilling pattern of corporate espionage, and the explosive truth about her own identity comes to light, her survival is at stake. Because in the Montague family, secrets don't stay hidden. They get eliminated.
