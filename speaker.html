The Speaker

The power of a story is knowing what makes people care.

Understand the audience, find the story that matters, and make people care.

Transforming ideas into lasting impact.

Gledé Browne Kabongo is an award-winning thriller author, strategic communications leader, and speaker. For more than 20 years, she has helped organizations communicate complex ideas with clarity and purpose.

Today, she brings that same strategic thinking, and the storytelling techniques behind her nationally recognized psychological thrillers, to speaking engagements, workshops, and masterclasses that help authors, marketers, leaders, and organizations communicate more effectively, build stronger brands, and create lasting impact.
