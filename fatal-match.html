The Story

She built a matchmaking empire. Then she matched a monster.

Harvard-trained psychologist Dr. Sabrina Ambrose built her Miami elite matchmaking empire on a near-perfect algorithm and a 98% success rate pairing the powerful, wealthy, and famous. But perfection can hide monsters.

When a devastated client accuses Sabrina of matching her with a man who seduces successful women only to destroy their careers, Sabrina starts digging, and uncovers a chilling pattern she somehow missed.

Dominic Blackwell is everything her female clients want: brilliant, wealthy, irresistible. And devastatingly dangerous to ambitious women. When Sabrina confronts him, Dominic doesn’t deny anything. Instead, he delivers a threat of his own: back off or he will expose the shameful secret that nearly ended Sabrina’s career.

Then Dominic is found murdered. And the police are asking questions. Evidence points straight at Sabrina. As her carefully built empire begins to crack, she realizes someone else has been keeping score. Someone who knows what Dominic did and was willing to stop him before he ruined another woman.

Perfect for fans of twisty psychological thrillers, dangerous secrets, and powerful women pushed to the edge, Fatal Match will keep you turning pages late into the night.
