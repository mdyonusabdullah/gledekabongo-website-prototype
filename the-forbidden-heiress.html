The Story

There are two ways to bury secrets. Six feet under or beneath billions of dollars.

  • 01Power
  • 02Identity
  • 03Secrets

Drowning in grief and desperate for work, cancer researcher Sabree Warner leaps at the chance to join Montague Pharma, one of the world’s most powerful pharmaceutical dynasties. Her assignment: find out why promising treatments were mysteriously shelved before they could save lives.

But someone doesn’t want her asking questions. A car tries to run her down. Her apartment is ransacked. The abandoned drugs weren’t shelved by mistake—they were buried to hide a devastating secret that could bring down the entire Montague empire.

Then Sabree discovers her connection to the Montague family runs deeper—and deadlier—than any corporate conspiracy. As a vicious succession battle rages, someone is watching her. Someone who has already killed to keep the truth about her identity buried.

Now Sabree’s not just fighting for answers, she’s fighting to stay alive. Because in the Montague family, secrets don’t stay hidden—they get eliminated.
