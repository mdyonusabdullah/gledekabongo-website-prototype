The Story

New name. New life. Perfect family.

She reinvented herself from the ground up. New name. New life. Perfect family. For twenty-two years, Dr. Shelby Cooper has been safe—or so she believed. But a fresh start built on deception has a shelf life, and Shelby’s is about to expire. Her perfect life is about to come crashing down when she’s arrested for murder. But her nightmare is just beginning. An ingenious killer is out for revenge, and won’t stop until Shelby is irrevocably ruined.
