Meet Abbie Cooper

She’s brilliant, ambitious, and impossible to break.

Meet Abbie Cooper, a high achiever with a meticulously planned future and the kind of nerve it takes to stare down her worst enemies.

But when deception, obsession, and carefully orchestrated revenge start dismantling everything she’s built, Abbie doesn’t run. She fights back.

From the halls of a cutthroat high school to the shadowy corridors of academia, from a neighborhood hiding dangerous secrets to a marriage under siege — the Fearless Series follows one unforgettable woman across four heart-pounding thrillers. Each book delivers jaw-dropping twists, shocking secrets, simmering tension, and a heroine who will not be broken.

01

Game of Fear

One dirty secret. One wicked game. One traitor among them.

Haunted by a mistake she’s desperate to keep hidden, high school senior Abbie Cooper takes on a determined stalker bent on exposing her secret—a secret that could shatter her Ivy League dreams. But Abbie isn’t about to let anyone ruin her future, so it’s game on.

02

Autumn of Fear

The perfect girl. The perfect trap. The perfect crime.

As her second year at Yale University begins, Abbie Cooper is one step closer to achieving her goal of becoming one of the world’s top neurosurgeons. But that dream is upended when she wakes up in the hospital after a violent assault and no memory of the attack. To uncover the truth of what happened that night, Abbie must confront a stunning web of lies that stretch back decades, and a vicious predator who’s willing to kill to protect his secret.

Learn More →

03

Winds of Fear

Perfect neighbors. Perfect liars.

Stay-at-home mom and Ph.D. candidate Abbie Rambally suspects something is terribly wrong when the Paynes, her charming new neighbors, take an unnerving interest in her son. To keep her family safe and uncover what her neighbors are hiding, she must look to her traumatic past and long-buried secrets for answers before it’s too late.

Reign of Fear

Fatal Attraction meets The Last Mrs. Parrish

When Kristina Hayward, her husband’s glamorous ex reappears, determined to settle old scores and dismantle the life she’s built, Abbie crosses unthinkable lines to protect her family and her marriage.
