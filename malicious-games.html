Book One Lucas Rambally Out Now

A Game of Malice

When tech prodigy Mackenzie Fleming vanishes, Lucas Rambally uncovers a dangerous game of secrets, strategy, and betrayal lurking beneath the academy’s polished façade. But the deeper he digs, the clearer it becomes: someone at Branson has been playing a long, calculated game, and Mackenzie’s disappearance was only the opening move.

Book Two Alexis Rambally

A Mind of Malice

Alexis Rambally becomes the target of a vicious campaign led by the school’s untouchable queen bee. What begins as brutal social warfare soon reveals a sinister conspiracy tied to powerful corporations and a program designed to identify the world’s most brilliant young minds—at any cost.

Coming in 2027

Book Three Blake Rambally

A Legacy of Malice

When a star athlete dies under suspicious circumstances, Blake Rambally discovers that Branson’s relentless drive to win may have pushed someone past the point of no return. As envy, ambition, and buried secrets collide, the truth threatens to shatter the academy’s carefully constructed reputation, permanently.
