Wealth. Privilege. Deadly secrets.

Brilliant Caribbean-American women navigate the city’s most exclusive circles, where beautiful lives hide dangerous secrets, and the powerful will do anything to keep them buried.

The Miami Elite Thrillers are a collection of addictive psychological thrillers set among the city’s most powerful circles—power brokers who believe their wealth makes them untouchable. Perfect for readers who love twisty psychological thrillers, powerful women, and dangerous secrets.
