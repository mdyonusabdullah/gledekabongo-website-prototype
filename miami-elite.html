The Series

The Miami Elite Novellas are a collection of addictive psychological thrillers set among the city’s most powerful circles—power brokers who believe their wealth makes them untouchable. Each story follows a brilliant Caribbean-American heroine navigating spaces where privilege is currency and truth is dangerous.

Perfect for readers who love twisty psychological thrillers, powerful women, and dangerous secrets, the Miami Elite Novella Series will keep you turning pages late into the night.

Four women, four ways in

  • 01 A matchmaker whose perfect algorithm hides a predator. Fatal Match
  • 02 A concierge who knows the secrets the rich want to keep buried at all costs. The Last Concierge
  • 03 An art curator who discovers a masterpiece worth killing for.
  • 04 A reputation fixer protecting the powerful—until the truth turns on her.

Fatal Match

Perfectly charming. Perfectly deadly.

She built a matchmaking empire. Then she matched a monster.

Harvard-trained psychologist Dr. Sabrina Ambrose built her Miami elite matchmaking empire on a near-perfect algorithm and a 98% success rate pairing the powerful, wealthy, and famous. But perfection can hide monsters.

When a devastated client accuses Sabrina of matching her with a man who seduces successful women only to destroy their careers, Sabrina starts digging, and uncovers a chilling pattern she somehow missed. Dominic Blackwell is everything her female clients want: brilliant, wealthy, irresistible. And devastatingly dangerous to ambitious women. When Sabrina confronts him, Dominic doesn’t deny anything. Instead, he delivers a threat of his own: back off or he’ll expose the Boston scandal that nearly ended Sabrina’s career.

Then Dominic is found murdered. Now the police are asking questions. And as her carefully built empire begins to crack, Sabrina realizes someone else has been keeping score. Someone who knows what Dominic did and was willing to stop him before he ruined another woman.

Because sometimes the perfect match…is a deadly mistake.

The Last Concierge

She knows their darkest secrets. Now they want her silenced.

Every client has a secret. One was worth killing for.

Julie-Ann Shillingford built Miami’s most exclusive concierge firm on one promise: discretion above all else. For eleven years, she’s guarded the secrets of the wealthy and powerful without asking questions. Until one of her clients is murdered.

Hours after the glittering Ashcroft Foundation Gala, philanthropist Isabel Dresden is found dead. The event had appeared flawless, but Julie-Ann remembers what lurked beneath the champagne and designer gowns: whispered confrontations, fractured loyalties, and a fear in Isabel she recognized too late.

As police close in on Isabel’s powerful husband, Julie-Ann quietly retraces her final hours, and discovers nearly everyone at the gala was hiding something. A husband with secrets. A business partner whose loyalty may be a lie. A mysterious hotelier with a carefully buried past.

Then Julie-Ann realizes someone is watching her. Someone believes Isabel trusted her with the truth. And if Julie-Ann uncovers what really happened twenty-five years ago, one of Miami’s most powerful families will lose everything.

Including the killer.
