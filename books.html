Brilliant cancer researcher Sabree Warner is thrilled when she's recruited by the powerful CEO of Montague pharma. But when she uncovers a chilling pattern of corporate espionage, and the explosive truth about her own identity comes to light, her survival is at stake. Because in the Montague family, secrets don't stay hidden. They get eliminated.

02 Fearless Series

Fearless Series

Meet Abbie Cooper.

She's brilliant, ambitious, and impossible to break.

Across four pulse-pounding psychological thrillers, deception, obsession, and revenge threaten everything she's fought for, and anyone who underestimates her does so at their peril.

From the halls of a cutthroat high school to the shadowy corridors of academia, from a neighborhood hiding dangerous secrets to a marriage under siege — the Fearless Series follows one unforgettable woman across four heart-pounding thrillers.

03 Miami Elite Thrillers

Miami Elite

Wealth. Privilege. Deadly secrets.

The Miami Elite Thrillers are a collection of addictive psychological thrillers set among the city's most powerful circles—power brokers who believe their wealth makes them untouchable. Each story follows a brilliant Caribbean-American heroine navigating spaces where privilege is currency and truth is dangerous.

Published May 2026

Fatal Match

She built a matchmaking empire. Then she matched a monster.

Harvard-trained psychologist Dr. Sabrina Ambrose built her Miami elite matchmaking empire on a near-perfect algorithm and a 98% success rate pairing the powerful, wealthy, and famous. But perfection can hide monsters.
